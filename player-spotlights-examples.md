# Handley Cup Countdown — Player Spotlight Examples

Six examples to show the format working across very different kinds of careers — a
serial overachiever, a one-and-done legend, a slow-burn climber, Mr. Reliable, a
nearly-man, and a breakout run. Each one follows the same shape so they're easy to
batch-produce for the other 71 players:

**Hook line → Career snapshot → Signature moment/win → Sign-off stat**

Swap in a photo (you'll have most of these already) and these are close to
post-ready as captions.

---

## Todd Haynes — "Super Todd"

**#4 Power Ranking · 8 appearances · Parson's Plate Champion 23/24**

Super Todd has been in the mix for longer than almost anyone in the field, and
2023/24 was the year it finally paid off — a Plate run from the quarter-final
on, beating Harvey Thompson, then Nathan Stewart 4-1 in the semi, then Finley
Sanderson 5-2 in the final to end a **6-year trophy drought**, the longest in
Handley Cup history. What really sets Todd apart though: he's faced **34
different opponents** across his 8 years — more than anyone else who's ever
played, including Charlie Marshall himself. If you've entered the Handley Cup
at any point since 18/19, there's a decent chance you've drawn Todd at some
stage.

*Stat to drop in the caption: most unique opponents faced in Handley Cup history.*

## Bart Timmer — "The Dart"

**#24 Power Ranking · 1 appearance · Cup Runner-Up 21/22**

One tournament. One legend. Bart Timmer turned up in 2021/22, went unbeaten
through his group (beating Alex Wright, Nathan Hemmingway, Todd Haynes and
Jamie Flint along the way), then knocked out Curtis Thorpe and Deck Ingber back
to back to reach the Cup Final — where he pushed Charlie Marshall to 5-3 before
falling just short. He's never played the Handley Cup since. **The only non-UK
player to ever reach a Cup Final, and the only Dutchman to play at all** —
genuinely one of the great one-off cameos in tournament history. Someone tell
Callum Woodcock to find his number.

*Stat to drop in the caption: only overseas player ever to reach a Handley Cup Final.*

## Dan Rushton — "Rushy"

**#25 Power Ranking · 3 appearances · Best run: Cup Quarter-Final 25/26**

Rushy plays the long game — literally. He holds the record for the **longest gap
between Handley Cup appearances (4 events)**, but when he's shown up, he's shown
up: 8 career 180s, a 130 checkout, and a tied-fastest leg on record at 13 darts.
2025/26 was his best campaign yet, going unbeaten through the group (5-0,
including statement wins over Hugo Nilsson and Morgan Shillito) before beating
Curtis Thorpe 4-3 in a Last 16 thriller to reach his first ever Cup quarter-final.
Watch this space.

*Stat to drop in the caption: tied for the fastest recorded leg in tournament history — 13 darts.*

## Curtis Thorpe — "Thorpedo"

**#6 Power Ranking · 8 appearances · Best run: Cup Semi-Final 19/20**

Thorpedo is the definition of Mr. Reliable — one of only **four players to have
attended every single Handley Cup**, and he's never once missed the knockouts.
What makes his record genuinely unusual: all 8 of his career knockout exits have
come against a **different opponent every single time** — no one's ever beaten
him twice. His deepest run came in 19/20, going all the way to a semi-final
against Jamie Flint. A 69.58 average and a 122 checkout on the stat sheet, and
he's beaten four different players in the knockouts along the way, most recently
dumping out Finley Sanderson in 25/26.

*Stat to drop in the caption: the only player never beaten twice by the same opponent — in 8 years of trying.*

## Deck Ingber — "Double Deckker"

**#7 Power Ranking · 7 appearances · Best run: Cup Semi-Final 21/22**

Double Deckker has been quietly one of the most consistent players in Handley
Cup history — 7 appearances, a Cup semi-final to his name, and a 60.4 average
and 125 checkout on the board. His best run in 21/22 saw him beat Todd Haynes
3-1 in the quarter-final before running into the Bart Timmer cameo tour, losing
4-0 in the semi. Fun fact: he holds the record for **the most appearances by
anyone with only a single Unique Performance award** — proof that consistency
and highlight reels don't always go hand in hand.

*Stat to drop in the caption: 7 Handley Cups, 1 semi-final, and he's beaten a Plate winner along the way (Todd Haynes, 21/22).*

## Oliver Wilson — "Wicked"

**#22 Power Ranking · 3 appearances · Best run: Plate Runner-Up 24/25**

Wicked's breakout came in 2024/25 — a Parson's Plate run for the ages, beating
Joe Evans, Lewis Ruston, Alex McCowen and Dan Harrison to reach the final,
before falling 2-4 to eventual champion Hugo Nilsson. A 61.42 average, a 119
checkout and a couple of 180s to his name — and with 3 appearances already
under his belt, there's a title-shaped hole in his record he'll fancy filling
next time out.

*Stat to drop in the caption: beat four players in a row to reach the 24/25 Parson's Plate Final.*

## Notes on scaling this to 30–50 days

- Every fact above is pulled directly from `players-data.js`, `events-data.js`,
  `power-rankings-data.js` and `records-data.js` — nothing's invented, so these
  are safe to post as-is.
- The strongest posts lean on something **specific and slightly surprising**
  (Timmer's one-and-done Final run, Thorpe's "never beaten twice" record) rather
  than just reciting the stat line — worth hunting for that angle per player.
- For players with thinner records (1-2 appearances, no deep runs), lean harder
  on nicknames, nationality "onlys", or a single good win rather than trying to
  force a career arc that isn't there yet.
- If you want, I can build a proper "spotlight generator" — feed it a player ID
  and it drafts the stat block + a rough paragraph automatically, pulling from
  the same data these six used. Wouldn't write the polished hook line for you
  (that's the fun part), but it'd save a lot of the digging for the other 71.
